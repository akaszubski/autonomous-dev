## 1. Mission

autonomous-dev exists to make Claude Code follow the full SDLC via deterministic
enforcement — hooks that hard-block, pipeline sequencing, alignment gates. The open
backlog has grown to 108 issues, and a meaningful fraction are cases where **the
harness fails to enforce itself** (commit-message guard bypassable, remediation
approval self-attestable, deny signals missing from the activity log). Those are
existential for a product whose entire value proposition is trustworthy enforcement.
This campaign triages the full queue, consolidates duplicate/root-shared issues,
closes what shipped work already fixed, and drains the high-ROI clusters —
enforcement holes first. It ties directly to the operating agreement's north star:
a durable toolkit whose guarantees actually hold.

## 7. Abort Conditions

- **A regression escapes to master** (a shipped enforcement fix breaks a green test
  on `master`) → STOP draining, root-cause the regression, do not continue the queue
  until master is green. Pivot: hotfix or revert.
- **Triage reveals the clusters are not consolidatable** (issues are genuinely
  independent, no shared root cause) → reduce scope to enforcement-holes only,
  re-plan the rest as individual `/implement` runs across sessions.
- **Any fix requires a paid action** → STOP that item, surface to user, do not
  proceed (mission $0 constraint).
- **Context saturation mid-cluster** → `/clear` or `/compact` and resume from this
  goal doc + task list; never ship a half-validated cluster.
